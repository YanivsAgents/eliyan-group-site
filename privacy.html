Privacy Policy

What we collect when you request a quote, book an assessment or text us, and what we do with it.

Effective September 30, 2026

Eliyan Group ("we," "us") builds concrete, asphalt and masonry projects in South Florida. This policy explains what we collect on www.eliyangroup.com and how we use it.

01What we collect

  • What you give us: your name, phone number, email, jobsite address, the service and timeline you are interested in, your project description, and the appointment you book. If you start a form and do not finish it, we may save what you have entered so we can follow up.
  • What we collect automatically: IP address, device and browser type, and the pages you view and buttons you click, through cookies and Google Tag Manager.
  • From Facebook and Instagram: if you request a quote through one of our ads, Meta sends us the details you entered.

02How we use it

To reply to you, prepare your written quote, schedule and remind you about your assessment, complete your project, and measure and improve our website and advertising.

03Text messages

When you check the text-message box, book an appointment, or text us, you agree to receive texts from Eliyan Group about your project. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

We do not share your mobile phone number or your text-messaging opt-in with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

04Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the providers that run our website, CRM, scheduling, messaging and advertising (HighLevel, Vercel, Google, Meta); the agency that manages our marketing; the crew and trades who quote and build your project; and when the law requires it.

05Cookies and advertising

We use Google Analytics, Google Ads and Meta Pixel to understand how visitors use the site and whether our ads lead to quote requests. You can block cookies in your browser and opt out of ad personalization at adssettings.google.com, in your Facebook ad preferences, or at optout.aboutads.info.

06Your choices

Reply STOP to stop texts. Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. Email info@eliyangroup.com to access, correct or delete your information. We keep project records as long as needed for warranty, tax and legal purposes. We protect your data with reasonable safeguards, but no system is completely secure. Our services are for adults 18 and over.

07Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post the new effective date here.
